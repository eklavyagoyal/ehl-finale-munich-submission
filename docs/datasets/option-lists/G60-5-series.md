## Legend

Model columns (always in this order, as in the catalog):
`i5 eDrive40` · `i5 xDrive40` · `i5 M60 xDrive` (BEV) · `530e` · `530e xDrive` · `550e xDrive` (PHEV) · `520i` (petrol MHEV) · `520d` · `520d xDrive` · `540d xDrive` (diesel MHEV).

| Symbol in this file | Catalog symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `S` | ■ | Serienausstattung (standard) |
| `O` | □ | Sonderausstattung (optional, at the price in the "Price" column of that row) |
| `–` | empty cell | Not offered for this model (the catalog prints no symbol; it has no explicit "not available" sign) |
| `·` | empty cell in a price-variant sub-row | No statement in this sub-row — look at the other row(s) of the same option |
| `–,–` | –,– | "Sonderausstattung ohne Mehr-/Minderpreis" (option at no extra cost) |
| `Serie` | Serie | Standard, printed instead of a price |
| `↳` | — | Price-variant sub-row of the option above (the catalog prints a second/third price line for some models or for combinations, e.g. "In Verbindung mit M Sportpaket") |

Reading rules:
- Availability of an option is the **union** of its main row and its `↳` sub-rows. A price applies only to the `O` cells of its own row (e.g. 3G0 is `S` on BEV/PHEV and `O` at 1.220,– on the diesel/petrol models).
- Colour/upholstery groups: the catalog prints one price and one row of symbols for a group of colours (e.g. six metallic paints). Here every colour gets its own row with the shared group price and symbols.
- "Nur in Verbindung mit" = only in combination with (requires); "Nicht in Verbindung mit" = not combinable with; "Im Umfang … enthalten" = included in.
- Prices are written in German number format as printed (`1.220,–` = EUR 1,220; `[1.025,21]` = net without VAT).
- Codes are BMW "SA-Nr." (Sonderausstattungsnummer) as printed. In the standard-equipment tables the catalog prints no codes; codes marked `¹` there are cross-referenced from another page of the same catalog where the identical item is listed with a code.

## Extraction notes

Method: text and the ■/□ glyphs were taken from the PDF text layer (`pdftotext -layout` and `-bbox`). Each glyph was assigned to a model column by its x-coordinate; all ten column positions are the same on every matrix page. The result was checked against the rendered pages 6–8, 9–11 and 13–19. No symbol was unreadable or ambiguous, so this file contains no `?` cells.

1. **Empty cells:** the catalog has only ■ (Serie) and □ (Sonderausstattung). An empty cell is shown as `–` (not offered); the catalog does not say this explicitly.
2. **Duplicate SA codes, as printed:** `416` is both the paint "M Carbonschwarz" (Farbwelt Exterieur) and "Sonnenschutzrollos" (Klimakomfort). `754` is "M Heckspoiler in Wagenfarbe" (option) and "M Heckspoiler in Black Sapphire" (M Sportpaket Pro). Neither could be resolved from the catalog.
3. **Parent vs colour codes:** `KS` (Veganza perforiert und gesteppt) and `VC` (BMW Individual Leder Merino) are printed as group codes above the colour codes KSFU/KSJX/KSSW and VCJL/VCJM/VCJN.
4. **4MA M Multifunktionssitze** requires "Leder Merino Schwarz/Schwarz oder Leder Merino Schwarz/Violett dunkel" (pages 9–11). Neither leather colour is listed in Farbwelt Interieur, which only has VCJL Schwarz \| Atlasgrau, VCJM Kupferbraun \| Atlasgrau and VCJN Silverstone II \| Atlasgrau. The catalog is inconsistent here. 4MA also has no availability matrix.
5. **Name variants for the same code:** KTSW is printed as "M Veganza/Alcantara Schwarz" (standard-equipment page), "M Alcantara/Veganza-Kombination Schwarz" (M Sportpaket page) and "M Alcantara \| Veganza-Kombination \| Schwarz" (Farbwelt Interieur). "M Dachhimmel anthrazit/Anthrazit" (775, M package) is a different item from "M Dachhimmel Alcantara anthrazit" (776, option). In "954 I Bicolor" and "Silverstone II I Atlasgrau" the capital letter I is printed where elsewhere a "\|" separator or "Individual" would be expected. Both are kept as printed.
6. **Net-price rounding differences, as printed:** 4MA is [1.470,59] on pages 9–10 but [1.470,58] on page 11. 71C is [1.596,63] on page 9 but [1.596,64] on page 17.
7. **M Sportpaket price lines:** the 4.600,– line has no model qualifier. That it applies to 520i/520d/520d xDrive/540d xDrive is an inference by elimination. No price is printed for i5 M60 xDrive.
8. **Package contents without prices:** on the M Sportpaket / M Sportpaket Pro / i5 M60 xDrive pages, items without a price are read as included. Items with a price (71C, 3M1, 4MA, 6F4) are read as extra-cost options. This is an interpretation of the layout.
9. **Price-variant rows without a label** (3G3 300,–; 3GN 850,–; 3GQ 1.200,–; 3GV 2.990,–/2.550,–; 71C 1.800,–; 776 1.200,–; 7VB 1.150,–): the catalog prints only a second price aligned with the symbols of certain models. These rows are labelled "↳ price for the marked models".
10. **Consumption values differ between sections.** The price pages give one homologation value (e.g. i5 M60 xDrive 19,5 – 19,4 kWh/100 km). The technical pages give the configuration range (19,5 – 17,1). Both are kept as printed. The same applies to the PHEV CO2 class: price page "F/B" (discharged/combined) vs technical page "B" combined and "F–E" discharged.
11. **BEV AC charging:** printed as "11 / 22⁴" and "8:30 / 4:15⁴", where footnote 4 = "Serienausstattung/Sonderausstattung". The i5 M60 xDrive shows "22 / –⁴" for power but still "8:30 / 4:15" for charging time, as printed. The HPC row cites footnote 7, which is not printed on the BEV page; footnote 3 (at "Batterie / Laden") covers the HPC value.
12. **Abgasnorm:** printed as "EU6e bis" for the PHEVs (apparently truncated) and "EU6e bis FCM" for petrol/diesel. Transcribed as printed.
13. **The BEV technical page has no non-electric "Höchstgeschwindigkeit" row**, only "Höchstgeschwindigkeit elektr.".
14. **Page references:** "siehe Seite 17" (Innovationspaket, Comfort Paket) refers to the catalog's own page numbering. Parking Assistant Professional (5DW) and Komfortsitze (456) are on PDF page 18.
15. **Small source oddities kept as printed:** "Active Park Distance Control" appears twice in the standard Parking Assistant list. "Mänoverassistent" is spelled that way. The standard-equipment page gives tyres as "245/45 R19", the wheel page as "245/45".
16. **Dimension labels are inferred.** The drawings print bare numbers. Length, wheelbase, overhangs, height and the two widths can be identified from position with good confidence. Track widths are fairly confident. The diagonal 1033/969 (headroom) and top-view 1536/1497 (interior width, possibly shoulder or elbow width) are the least certain.
17. **Standard equipment is "ausgewählt" (selected).** The catalog does not claim the list is complete.

18. **Option-code count:** the SA-Nr. columns on pages 9–11 and 13–19 contain 79 distinct code strings. That is 77 if the parent codes KS and VC are left out. 416 and 754 each count once even though each stands for two items (see note 2). All 79 strings appear in this file. The standard-equipment pages also list items with no code at all (e.g. Launch Control, Ambientes Licht, Driving Assistant); these are transcribed without a code.
