# Competitor analysis: BMW 5 Series (G60, China G68)

Which vehicles the 5 Series successor competes with in **EU, US and China**, and what their customers and the press say about them. All findings use the **same theme axis as BMW's own feedback**, so BMW and competitors can be compared theme by theme.

Collected on 2026-10-10 by 8 web-research agents plus 1 classification run over BMW's G60 verbatims. Data, schema and scripts are in [`/competitors`](../../competitors/README.md).

Why these: German premium rivals are the classic cross-shops. Tesla is the brand BMW G60 customers name most (66 of 191 verbatims that mention a rival). Genesis, Lexus and Cadillac are the US value alternatives. Volvo ES90 and NIO ET7 are what European press tests against the i5. In China, Chinese NEV sedans in the same price band are positioned against the 5 Series Li.

## How to read the comparison

Comparisons are only made **within the same channel**:

| Channel | BMW side | Competitor side | Comparable? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owner voice | G60 **Source A** owner reviews (EU/US) · G68 Sohu owner reviews (CN) | owner reviews / forums from the web | Directional. In CN it is like-for-like (same platform). In EU/US the platforms differ, so the scorecard also gives each theme's critical share **relative to that side's average** |
| Head-to-head verdicts | BMW verbatims that compare with a rival | press tests and owners that compare with the 5 Series / i5 | Yes. Counts who is judged better on a theme |
| Complaints (US) | NHTSA complaints, 5 Series MY2024+ | NHTSA complaints, 9 rivals | Yes, same source and method. Compare shares; volumes depend on fleet size |
| Press | BMW option list (DE), BMW US/CN specs | tests and spec pages | Equipment facts, 20 fixed features |

"Critical" = Wants + Difficult to Use + Defect (everything that is not a Like). Treat all shares as signals: most themes have 5–60 statements per side.

### EU

Owner voice is thin on both sides (BMW EU Source A: ~160 statements; competitors ~100), so **press verdicts carry the market**:

- **i5 wins both EV comparison tests**: vs EQE 350 (AUTO BILD, 635 : 612) and vs A6 e-tron Avant (AUTO ZEITUNG, 3,305 : 3,292). It wins on handling, boot, rear seat posture and price.
- **Rivals win on ride comfort and quietness**: E-Class estate (air suspension) beat the 520d Touring 3,322 : 3,294. EQE measured quieter (61 vs 63 dB), and the A6 e-tron's air suspension rides better than the i5 Touring.
- **Charging gap**: ES90 (800 V, ~300 kW, 10–80 % in 22 min) and A6 e-tron (270 kW, 23 min) vs i5 at 205 kW / 30 min. EU verdicts on charging 5 : 3 for rivals; Charging is the biggest BMW EU owner theme with a high critical share (26 statements, 0.73 critical vs 0.14 for rivals).
- **Touch-only controls are the shared weakness of every rival**: no physical climate controls on E-Class, A6, A6 e-tron, ES90 or Teslas. AUTO ZEITUNG prefers iDrive's controller to Mercedes touch controls. Audi is bringing back steering-wheel rollers for MY2026.
- **Audi interior quality has slipped** (ADAC, AUTO ZEITUNG: hard plastics, piano black, rattles).

### China

The cleanest owner-voice comparison: G68 and its rivals are all reviewed on Sohu Auto.

- **Equipment value is the G68's weak spot, and rivals win every head-to-head**: verdicts 7 : 0 for rivals. G68 owners complain about cut equipment by trim: no ventilated seats on 525Li/530Li entry trims, sunroof that cannot open, stripped audio, paid remote start / remote climate, 3 months of free data. Audi A6L owners call their car better value. Chinese NEVs ship driver assistance and ventilated seats as standard where the i5 charges extra.
- **Design**: verdicts 6 : 0 for rivals. E-Class L owners rate its styling above the new 5 Series, and some did not consider the BMW because of its looks.
- **Driver assistance**: Huawei ADS (Luxeed S7, Stelato S9) and Zeekr highway assist are the most praised feature among NEV owners (rival relative critical share −0.22). Lidar is standard on SU7, 007 and S7, and the A6L e-tron uses Huawei NOA. The G68 offers L2 only, and China approved its first L3 cars in December 2025.
- **Charging**: 800–900 V is the norm in the class (Zeekr 007 10–80 % in ~10 min, Luxeed S7 30–80 % in ~12 min) vs i5 L 30–80 % in ~36 min.
- **Where the German trio is all weak**: high rear centre tunnel (G68, E-Class L, A6L). G68: brake squeal/judder (4 of 15 reviewers on one trim page), body roll (no rear anti-roll bar, confirmed by the Autohome test). A6L C8: jerky at low speed (11 owners). E-Class L: rattles and new-car odour.

## Data quality and caveats

- **Thin owner voice outside China and US review sites.** EU owner forums and review sites are mostly blocked (Parkers, Honest John, motor-talk, Reddit, Edmunds, What Car, many forums behind paid bot gateways). Volvo ES90, Tesla Model S (EU), Audi A6 C9 and the 8th-gen Lexus ES have press records only.
- **Press dominates EU.** About 140 EU records come from ADAC alone. Count reviews (`review_ref`), not records.
- **Comparisons are not always against the G60.** Some refer to the G61 Touring, i4, i7 or the previous-generation G30; the statement names the model.
- **NHTSA**: almost all complaints are defects by design. Buckets are noisy (2023 EQE mixes sedan and SUV; text filter applied). Tesla's volumes reflect its fleet size, so compare shares.
- **China**: all owner reviews come from Sohu trim pages (Autohome renders reviews via JavaScript). Sohu snippets lean positive, and most reviews predate the latest updates (SU7 2026, ADS 4).
- **Conflicts to keep in mind**: Tesla Model S end of production (sources say May 2026 or late 2026), trunk volumes that differ between sources, and the ET7's CarPlay (catalogue says standard, tests say not available).
- **Quotes** come from WebFetch summaries and are not guaranteed verbatim; quotes ≥ 15 words were removed.
- BMW verbatim text is never stored, only IDs and labels.
