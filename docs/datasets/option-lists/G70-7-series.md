# BMW 7 Series (G70) — Equipment & price list (DE, July 2026)

> Source: `data/G70_OptionList.PDF`, "BMW 7er Katalog Juli 2026" (cover: "BMW 7 Limousine, Juli 2026"), Ausgabe 1, 28.05.2026, 27 pages. German market. Prices are in EUR incl. 19 % VAT; the net price without VAT follows in [brackets], as printed. Prices are the manufacturer's non-binding recommended retail prices ex works, without delivery costs ("unverbindliche Preisempfehlung ab Werk ohne Überführungskosten"). Option names are kept in German as printed. Pricing is out of challenge scope but can serve as a rough cost/effort signal.

**Page map:** p. 1 cover · p. 2 contents · p. 3 divider · pp. 4–5 Fahrzeugpreise · pp. 6–9 Ausgewählte Serienausstattungen · p. 10 M Sportpaket · p. 11 M Sportpaket Pro · p. 12 i7 M70 xDrive · p. 13 divider · pp. 14–15 Räder und Reifen · p. 16 Farbwelt Exterieur · pp. 17–19 Farbwelt Interieur (Polster, Interieurleisten, Lenkräder) · pp. 20–22 Sonderausstattungen · p. 23 divider · pp. 24–25 Technische Werte · p. 26 BMW Welt Automobilabholung · p. 27 recycling note / copyright.

**Model coverage caveat:** The price list (pp. 4–5) has **7 models**. Every equipment, option, colour and wheel matrix (pp. 6–22) has only **4 columns**: i7 50xDrive, i7 60xDrive, 740 xDrive and i7 M70 xDrive. The catalog does not state equipment or option availability for the **750e xDrive, M760e xDrive or 740d xDrive**, and it has no technical-data page for them. Do not assume they match the 740 xDrive.

## Extraction notes

**Method.** Text came from the PDF text layer (pdftotext / PyMuPDF). Every availability symbol (■ / □) was assigned to a model column from its x-coordinate: the column centres sit at x ≈ 447 / 469 / 490 / 511 pt on all matrix pages, so the mapping is unambiguous. All matrix pages (6–9, 14–22) were also checked visually at 200 dpi, and no discrepancies were found.

**Ambiguous / not reliably readable (marked `?`):**
1. **p. 17, BMW Individual Leder 'Merino' (VCSW, VCMY, VCFU, VCTQ).** The four colours are one block with one main price (2.500,– [2.100,84], □ for i7 50/60/740). On the line of the 4th colour, "Tartufo VCTQ", the catalog prints "–,–" with a □ only under i7 M70. Two readings are possible:
   - (a) this is the i7 M70 price for the whole colour group, as with other second price lines in the catalog;
   - (b) Tartufo is offered only on the M70, at no extra cost.
   
   The M70 availability of VCSW/VCMY/VCFU and the i7 50/60/740 availability of VCTQ are therefore `?`.
2. **p. 17, BMW Individual mit Leder 'Merino' mit Exklusivumfängen (VDSW, VDMY, VDFU, ZDHV, VDTQ).** The main price is 11.100,– [9.327,73] (□ for i7 50/60/740). On the line of "Adelaide Grau ZDHV" the catalog prints 10.700,– [8.991,60] with a □ only under i7 M70. "Tartufo VDTQ" follows on a separate line with no price and no symbols. The readings are:
   - Is 10.700,– the M70 price for the whole group, or is ZDHV M70-only?
   - Which price/availability applies to VDTQ?
   
   These cannot be decided. Note also that p. 19 (255 Sport-Lederlenkrad) mentions "BMW Individual Leder 'Merino' mit Exklusivumfängen/Tartufo und Caramel Atlasgrau".
3. **p. 17, ZDIE (Caramel/Atlasgrau) and ZETM (Schwarz/Grau).** They are printed under VEII (11.800,– / M70 11.400,–) in the same block, before the block's "In Verbindung mit BMW Individual Premiere Collection 700,–" line, but they have no price or symbols of their own. Their price and availability are `?`.
4. **Interior trim standard on the i7 M70 xDrive is contradictory:**
   - p. 6 lists "Interieurleisten Edelholz Fineline schwarz mit Metalleffekt hochglänzend, M Signatur" as standard (■) for the M70 only, and no code for it appears anywhere.
   - p. 12 and p. 18 instead show 43B "M Interieurleiste(n) Carbon Fibre …" as Serie / ■ for the M70.
5. **Radschraubensicherung (2PA) is contradictory:**
   - p. 8 (standard equipment) shows ■ only for the i7 M70 xDrive.
   - p. 15 shows ■ for all 4 models, with "Nur in Verbindung mit optionalen Rädern und Reifen".
   
   Both are transcribed as printed.
6. **4D7 (p. 18):** price column "Serie" with ■ for i7 50/60/740 and □ for i7 M70. No price is printed for the M70 option.
7. **Contradictory symbol:** on p. 15, the 3R1 line "In Verbindung mit BMW Individual Premiere Collection und M Sportpaket Pro" has a □ under i7 M70 xDrive, although pp. 10–11 say the M Sportpaket Pro is "Nicht in Verbindung mit i7 M70 xDrive". Transcribed as printed.
